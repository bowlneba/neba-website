Review draft — not confirmed. Three directions for surfacing Truncated/Cancelled status on the tournament schedule list (TournamentPastCard/TournamentUpcomingCard), now that Tournament Detail shows this via a badge + eligibility note. Scroll to compare; nothing here is wired up yet.

Option A

Inline status pill

Smallest footprint: a colored pill sits in the existing eyebrow line next to the date, and the champion/results copy is swapped for a one-line explanation. Card chrome (opacity, logo) is untouched, so a dense list stays visually calm — a status hunter has to read the pill, a casual scanner barely notices.

Option B

Dimmed card + corner ribbon

Leans on peripheral vision: the whole card desaturates/dims (more than the existing past-card 0.92 opacity) and a diagonal ribbon crosses the logo corner, the way e-commerce sites flag "Out of Stock" or "Sold". Reads instantly while scanning a long list, at the cost of being a more disruptive visual than Option A — worth checking against a season with many entries to make sure it doesn't read as "broken."

Option C

Accent strip + column takeover

Reuses the Tournament Detail page's own language directly: a left accent strip (same amber/gray as the detail page's eligibility note) plus a compact copy of that note dropped into the champion/actions column, replacing whatever would normally render there. The most informative option — no click-through needed to learn why — at the cost of being the busiest of the three and needing its own condensed copy variant.
